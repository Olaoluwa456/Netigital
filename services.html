Delivery lifecycle

One disciplined
delivery framework.

Each engagement should have clear objectives, agreed scope, accountable owners and defined acceptance criteria.

01Discover

Understand users, context and needs.

02Design

Shape requirements and architecture.

03Build

Develop and integrate the solution.

04Validate

Test quality, safety and performance.

05Deliver

Deploy with training and documentation.

06Improve

Maintain, support and evolve.

Product philosophy

Build what earns its place.

Potential products may include subscription software, enterprise platforms, developer tools, connected systems and specialist digital products. A product should progress from concept to development only after customer discovery, feasibility assessment, security and privacy review, and a clear maintenance plan.

NETIGITAL should not describe a concept as a released product, or a prototype as a proven commercial solution.
