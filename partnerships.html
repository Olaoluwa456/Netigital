Partner selection

Trust is built through due diligence.

Before entering a partnership, NETIGITAL should assess the partner’s identity, reputation, technical capability, financial stability, security practices, legal standing and conflicts of interest. Agreements should clarify scope, intellectual property, confidentiality, data handling, service levels, liabilities and exit arrangements.

No partnership, endorsement or commercial relationship is claimed on this website.
