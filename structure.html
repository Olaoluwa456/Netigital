A flexible structure

Not every capability needs its own company.

NETIGITAL should choose the lightest structure that supports clear accountability and sound risk management. A capability may begin as an internal team, grow into a business unit or brand, and become a legally separate subsidiary only when there is a strong strategic, operational, financial or regulatory reason.

There is no predetermined maximum number of specialist areas or subsidiaries. Growth should follow evidence and readiness, not a target count.

When to create a subsidiary

Evidence before expansion.

A formal assessment should be completed before establishing a new legal entity or making a major structural change.

Distinct business case

A defined market, customer need and strategic reason for separation.

Financial viability

A credible plan for capital, operating costs, cash flow and sustainability.

Leadership & expertise

Qualified people with clear decision rights and accountability.

Legal & regulatory need

A justified structure that meets jurisdiction and industry obligations.

Operational readiness

Processes, controls, systems and risk management appropriate to the activity.

Group-level value

A clear contribution to the wider group without unnecessary complexity.
