CONTENTS
- index.html: Home page
- about.html: Company direction, vision, mission and values
- technology.html: Five groups and searchable technology-domain catalogue
- services.html: Potential products, services and delivery lifecycle
- innovation.html: Research and responsible innovation

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser. Most content works without a build step.
3. For best local testing, serve the folder with a local static web server.

HOW TO PUBLISH
Upload the contents of this folder to a static website host. Configure the domain and replace the example.invalid entries in sitemap.xml with the final verified domain. Update metadata and test all pages after publishing.

IMPORTANT STATUS AND CONTENT NOTES
NETIGITAL is presented as a proposed future business in preparation. This website does not claim that the company is incorporated, funded, staffed, licensed, accredited or currently trading. Technology areas are proposed areas of interest, not confirmation of current service capability.

The contact form is a front-end preview only. It validates basic fields but does not send or store submissions. Connect it to a secure, verified backend and publish a real business contact channel before accepting enquiries.

The logo image provided by the founder is included unchanged at assets/netigital-main-logo.jpg. Its artwork contains a registered-mark symbol; this package does not independently verify trademark registration. Obtain appropriate legal advice before relying on trademark status or making registration claims.
